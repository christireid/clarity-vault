# MARKETING POSTMORTEM

> Perspective: Killer Product Marketer

---

## Messaging Sharpness: D-

**Current tagline**: "Manage Your AI Prompts with Confidence"

Problems:
- "Confidence" is vague. Confidence in what?
- "Manage" is the most boring verb in SaaS
- No specificity about what pain this solves
- Could describe any of 50 competitor products

**Better alternatives:**
- "Stop losing your best prompts in Slack threads"
- "Git for prompts. Test, version, ship."
- "Your prompts are code. Treat them that way."

---

## Emotional Resonance: F

The landing page is technically competent HTML but emotionally dead:
- No pain point identification ("Are you copying prompts into Notion?")
- No urgency ("Your prompts break silently when models update")
- No aspiration ("Ship AI features with confidence")
- No story
- No human element

It reads like a specification document, not a product page.

---

## Differentiation Clarity: F

The features section lists 6 generic feature categories with generic descriptions:
1. Version Control - "Track changes, create branches, and rollback"
2. Variable System - "Never hardcode values in prompts again"
3. LLM Playground - "Compare outputs, track costs"
4. Evaluation Pipeline - "Automated testing and regression detection"
5. Team Collaboration - "Enterprise-grade access control"
6. Analytics - "Make data-driven decisions"

Every single one of these could be copy-pasted from any LLMOps landing page. There is zero differentiation signal.

**What's missing:**
- "Unlike X, we do Y"
- Specific technical depth showing expertise
- Opinionated stance on how prompt management should work
- Unique approach or methodology

---

## Website Clarity: C-

**Good:**
- Clean design
- Clear layout structure
- Custom color theme
- Dark mode support

**Bad:**
- "View on GitHub" links to github.com root
- "Start Free" leads to empty product
- No product screenshots or demos
- No pricing page
- Footer links to /privacy and /terms (404)
- No favicon/logo beyond the letter "P"
- No social proof section
- No comparison section
- No FAQ
- No "How it works" section

---

## Narrative Strength: F

There is no narrative. No story about:
- Why this exists
- Who built it and why they care
- What problem they personally experienced
- What the vision is

The closest thing to a narrative is the implementation strategy doc, which is an internal planning document not suitable for external consumption.

---

## Brand Credibility: F

- Name confusion: repo is "clarity-vault", product is "Prompt Vault"
- Generic brand identity (letter "P" in a box)
- No memorable visual identity
- promptvault.dev domain referenced but likely not live
- No social media presence
- "Prompt Vault Team" as author - no real people

---

## Recommendations

1. **Pick a name and stick with it.** "Prompt Vault" or "Clarity Vault" - not both.
2. **Add one screenshot** of the product actually working (build the thing first).
3. **Write a founder story** - even 2 sentences about why this exists.
4. **Add a "How it works" section** with 3 concrete steps.
5. **Fix all broken links** immediately.
6. **Remove features that don't exist** from the landing page.
7. **Add a real GitHub link** when you have something to show.
